## Tally
- OVER or probably OVER: 11 (Witcher 3, Phasmophobia, Valheim, Black Myth, ARC Raiders, Ready or Not, Crusader Kings III, Crimson Desert, Don't Starve Together, Bannerlord, Satisfactory).
- Starfield: PS5 overlay inflated; Forza Horizon 5: PS5 overlay modestly high.
- OK: 5 (Palworld, Stardew Valley, Red Dead Redemption 2, Monster Hunter: World, Hogwarts Legacy).
- Cannot judge or no usable public figure: 11.
- Protected: Space Marine 2 (Saber).

## What this means for the rule
- In the OVER cases where a recent first-party figure exists (ARC Raiders, Ready or Not, Phasmophobia), the native estimate is near the public figure and the overlay is far above it.
- Native is not reliable everywhere: Forza Horizon 5 PS5 native (2.2M) is far below the public 5 to 6M. A blanket switch to native would trade errors.
- Steam's own estimate exceeds the all-platform public total for Phasmophobia, Valheim and Black Myth. That is a Steam-side error and no overlay rule fixes it.
